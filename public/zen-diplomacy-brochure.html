07 / BEYOND THE COMMITTEE

EVERYTHING HAPPENS.
EVERYTHING GETS RECORDED.

ZEN.DIPLOMACY isn't limited to what happens inside the committee room. The conference will have a dedicated documentation and editorial ecosystem where the ideas, moments, debates and creative work produced throughout the conference can live beyond the two days.

DOCUMENTATION Committee proceedings, key moments, decisions and developments — documented throughout the conference.
EDITORIALS Independent youth perspectives, analysis and opinion pieces emerging from the discussions.
CARICATURES Creative visual commentary on the politics, personalities and moments of the conference.
ARTS Creative work inspired by the themes, debates and experiences of ZEN.DIPLOMACY.
SOLUTIONS Ideas, proposals and innovative approaches developed around the issues being discussed.
THE COMMITTEE ENDS. THE CONVERSATION DOESN'T.
All of this content will be uploaded and hosted on ZENVITRA, creating a lasting digital record of the conference.
